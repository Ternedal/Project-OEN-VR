# Provenance — A2 Camp production source pack 001

## Geometry

The four OBJ/MTL source meshes are original procedural authoring produced for
PROJECT ØEN from the corresponding `source_art/props/a2/*.md` requirements.
Generator: `tools/generate_camp_source_meshes.py`. No third-party mesh source is
embedded.

## Raster materials

Generated with Codex built-in ImageGen on 2026-08-14 as one 2×2 diffuse atlas,
then deterministically cropped to four 1024×1024 PNG files. The unmodified atlas
is retained as `textures/MAT_CAMP_ATLAS_SOURCE_001.png`.

Final prompt:

> Seamless game material texture atlas for a standalone Unity/Quest VR prop
> pack: four equal quadrants containing weathered tropical expedition wood,
> worn dark utility iron, charcoal-gray beach stones and aged off-white canvas;
> hand-painted stylized realism, neutral diffuse albedo, broad mobile-VR detail,
> no objects, text, logos, perspective, borders, directional light or watermark.

No human art approval, Unity integration, headset readability, shader tuning or
release approval is claimed by this provenance record.
