# Storm phase reference

| State | Wind | Rain | Debris | Fire visibility | Structural motion | Horizon |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S0 warning | low→medium | none/light | sparse | strong | occasional | pressure front |
| S1 wind | high gusts | light | light | strong | tarp/rope active | darkening |
| S2 rain/fire | high | heavy | light | critical focal | moderate | overcast |
| S3 consequence | high | heavy | selective | readable | branch-dependent | overcast |
| S4 collapse | peak/local | heavy | short impact | readable | peak/local | obscured but usable |
| S5 signal/dawn | easing | easing | sparse | strongest focal | settling | opening dawn |

Product rule: increase intensity primarily through combined world response and parameter changes, not a completely new VFX family per phase.
