## Product intent

The bundle family is deliberately large/readable for VR:

- resources read as one shared/grabbable unit
- silhouettes differ before labels are read
- no pixel-hunting for tiny inventory objects
- wood/fiber/herbs remain visually distinct
- food/general supplies do not rely on real-world packaging language
