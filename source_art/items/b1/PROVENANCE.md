## Origin

All source masters are project-original vector designs made specifically for PROJECT ØEN.

No real-world brands, stock icons, traced assets or third-party illustrations are used.

## Product intent

The resource bundle family is deliberately large/readable for VR:

- resources read as one shared/grabbable unit
- silhouettes differ before labels are read
- no pixel-hunting for tiny inventory objects
- wood/fiber/herbs remain visually distinct
- food/general supplies do not rely on real-world packaging language

`ITM_KNIFE_001` is a camp utility/fiber-crafting tool:

- short broad rounded working profile
- oversized readable grip, hand stop and lanyard-hole cue
- working edge is communicated through shape and placement, not color alone
- styling remains practical, weathered and consistent with the shared utility-tool family
- clean/dry and worn/wet appearance intent is shown in the source master

## Intended use

Source/reference for later 3D/world item production. Runtime meshes, colliders, materials, interaction logic, networking and optimization remain Claude work.
